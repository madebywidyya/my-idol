Tian Xiwei (田曦薇) is a Chinese actress born in Chongqing on 14 October 1997. She graduated from Shanghai Theatre Academy and began her screen career in 2018.

From youthful characters to historical fantasy and romance, her filmography shows a journey of growing roles and wider recognition.
